## Figure 9

Estimator concordance for the four primary exposure-outcome pairs. IVW odds ratios and 95% confidence intervals are shown; weighted-median and MR-Egger results are displayed as point estimates because full confidence intervals were unavailable in the summarized results.

## Figure 10

Diagnostic summary for the four primary exposure-outcome pairs. Status indicators summarize heterogeneity, MR-Egger intercept, MR-PRESSO outlier, Steiger, NOME, and leave-one-out findings. MR-PRESSO is treated as auxiliary sensitivity evidence.

## Figure 11

Exploratory sample-overlap scenario analysis. This proxy analysis illustrates sensitivity to hypothetical overlap; it is not a formal pair-specific correction and did not inform the primary conclusions.

## Figure 12

Multivariable MR sensitivity analysis for BMI and smoking initiation. Panel A compares univariable IVW estimates with mutually adjusted estimates. Panel B shows conditional-F sensitivity across assumed constant cross-exposure estimation-error correlations from rho = -0.90 to 0.90. BMI remained above the conventional F = 10 threshold throughout the grid, whereas smoking initiation remained below it. The grid is a covariance-assumption stress test and does not estimate the true covariance. Abbreviations: BMI, body mass index; IVDD, intervertebral disc disorders; IVW, inverse-variance weighted; MVMR, multivariable Mendelian randomization.

## Figure 13

Observed and MRlap-corrected standardized estimates for the four primary pairs. MRlap estimates are standardized sensitivity measures and were not exponentiated or interpreted as primary odds ratios.

## Figure 14

Data requirements for sex-specific MR. Matched sex-specific exposure and outcome summary statistics were unavailable; therefore, no sex-specific causal estimates or sex-difference claims are reported.
