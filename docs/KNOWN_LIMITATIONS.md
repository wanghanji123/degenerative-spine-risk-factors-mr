# Known limitations and prohibited overinterpretations

1. The study uses genetic liability proxies. Estimates are not direct effects of weight loss, smoking cessation, or a specific clinical intervention.
2. FinnGen outcomes are registry-coded diagnoses. They are not imaging-grade phenotypes and do not establish anatomical severity.
3. Analyses are predominantly based on European-ancestry GWAS and may not generalize across ancestries.
4. MR-PRESSO is auxiliary. Significant global tests indicate residual heterogeneity/pleiotropy concern but do not localize pleiotropic SNPs when no outliers are detected.
5. The sample-overlap scenario table is illustrative. It does not measure pair-specific participant overlap.
6. MRlap results are correction-based sensitivity estimates on a standardized scale and should not be exponentiated as the primary odds-ratio estimates.
7. Exact MVMR SNP-exposure covariance was not identifiable from public marginal summary statistics. The covariance grid is a stress test, not an estimator of the true covariance.
8. Smoking-initiation conditional instruments were weak in MVMR, limiting claims about a BMI-independent smoking effect.
9. Matched sex-specific FinnGen outcome summary statistics were unavailable. No sex-specific causal estimate or sex-difference claim is supported.
10. Alternative adiposity results provide triangulation but do not imply that every adiposity measure has an equally strong association.
