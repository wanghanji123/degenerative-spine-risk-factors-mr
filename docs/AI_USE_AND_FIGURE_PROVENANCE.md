# AI use and figure provenance

## Scope of AI assistance

OpenAI Codex/ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Anthropic Claude were used under author direction for language editing, manuscript-structure review, and critical feedback. Human authors remain responsible for the scientific design, source selection, statistical interpretation, reference verification, originality, and final submitted content. No AI system is an author.

OpenAI GPT Image 2 was used to create non-data-bearing illustration layers during preparation of Figures 5 and 7. All labels, arrows, numerical statements, evidence grades, and caveats were added or verified by the authors. Generative AI was not used to fabricate or alter numerical results, participant data, primary research images, radiology, microscopy, or other observed data.

## Figure provenance

- Figures 1-4, 6, and 8-14 are author-directed study diagrams or data displays assembled from this study's documented aggregate results and analysis logic.
- Figures 5 and 7 combine non-data-bearing GPT Image 2 illustration layers with author-directed, code-rendered scientific content.
- Previously published articles were reviewed for common figure functions and reporting conventions, but no published figure or table was copied, traced, or redistributed in this repository.
- Figure captions identify interpretive schematics and analyses that are illustrative rather than inferential.

## Submission gate

Publisher policies differ and change over time. Before submission, the authors must check the selected journal's current policy for generative-AI text, explanatory images, and graphical abstracts. If the policy does not permit the Figure 5 or Figure 7 AI-assisted layer, replace it with deterministic author-drawn/vector artwork while preserving the verified labels and scientific meaning. Update the manuscript disclosure and captions after any replacement.

The authors should retain the human-reviewed final figure, the pre-overlay AI layer where available, and the documented caption/disclosure as provenance records. Do not describe AI-assisted artwork as wholly hand-drawn.
